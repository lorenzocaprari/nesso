The nesso package and image ship two model files:

- model.onnx: onnx/model_quantized.onnx from
  https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, an ONNX export of
  https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2.
  Both repositories declare the Apache License 2.0 in their model card
  metadata.
- vocab.txt: from https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
  (Apache License 2.0).

The fetcher pins these files to immutable Hugging Face revisions and verifies
their SHA-256 digests:
- Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, revision
  751bff37182d3f1213fa05d7196b954e230abad9:
  - int8 onnx/model_quantized.onnx:
    afdb6f1a0e45b715d0bb9b11772f032c399babd23bfc31fed1c170afc848bdb1
  - fp32 onnx/model.onnx:
    759c3cd2b7fe7e93933ad23c4c9181b7396442a2ed746ec7c1d46192c469c46e
- sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, revision
  1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41, vocab.txt:
  07eced375cec144d27c900241f3e339478dec958f92fddbc551f295c992038a3

sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 is fine-tuned from
https://huggingface.co/nreimers/MiniLM-L6-H384-uncased, which declares the
MIT License in its model card metadata.
